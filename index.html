About

3D Environment Artist

I'm an Environment Artist specializing in stylized art, with a strong focus on handpainting and handpainted PBR. I'm experienced in the full industry-standard pipeline, from concept and blockout to sculpting, baking, texturing, rendering, and in-game integration. As a recent graduate, I've worked on numerous projects, often taking responsibility for both project management and team coordination. I also completed an internship on Paper Strike, working as the main Environment Artist alongside industry professionals, which strengthened my ability to work efficiently in a professional team. More recently, I took part in the ZugZug Mentorship, where I further developed my handpainting skills.

Highly motivated and eager to grow as an artist, I'm always looking for new challenges. Feel free to reach out by email if you have any questions about me or my work!
