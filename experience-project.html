Paper Strike is a 3v3 online game in which players take on the role of mages and draw spells to defeat their opponents. For this project, I had the opportunity to join the team as an intern and work as the main Environment Artist. I was in charge of completely remodeling and retexturing the initial map, which was originally a rough blockout with only a few colors. With the help of François-Xavier, the concept artist, I created all the textures and models. I also went beyond the initial scope of my assignment by bringing in my own feedback and suggestions to make sure the project turned out as great as possible.
