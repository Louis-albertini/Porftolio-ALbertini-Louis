Here is a fully handpainted sword I created over 6 weeks as part of the Handpainted Weapon Course at ZugZug Mentorship! With the incredible guidance of Alexa Zimnukhova and Roman Busels ,I was able to create this sword using the full classic pipeline, from shape language to modeling and texturing. This is my first handpainted project, but definitely not my last! This experience really inspired me and made me realize a lot of things about the way I approach my work. Thanks again to the entire ZugZug team! It's a truly great place to learn alongside talented people.
